Personal library · iPhone and iPad

Quotes from the pages you read.

Ibid. keeps the books you own and the passages you want to remember. Photograph a page, keep the lines that matter, and find them again with the page number beside them.

What it does

  • Capture a passage by photographing the page. The text is read on your device; you choose the sentences to keep.
  • Add a book by scanning its barcode, or enter your copy’s details yourself.
  • Cite it properly: share a passage as formatted text in Harvard, APA or Chicago style, or as an image card.
  • Take it with you: export to Markdown, plain text, CSV, JSON or PDF, or keep a copy in a Markdown notes vault.
  • Keep it safe with an optional backup to your own iCloud, or a single archive file you keep yourself.

Private by design

There is no account and no tracking. Your library stays on your device. When you look a book up, only its ISBN, or its title and author, goes to public library catalogues. Read the privacy policy.
